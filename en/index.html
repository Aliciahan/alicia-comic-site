The manga reader for foldables

Read your own library: CBZ, EPUB, PDF, image folders, or a Komga / OPDS server on your NAS. Even old low-res scans keep their screentones and lines.

Version 1.0 is in TestFlight testing. The App Store link will appear here at launch.

  • HD

    The app’s own manga upscaling model enlarges low-resolution black-and-white pages without blurring screentones or lines. The first time, pick “Print-like” or “Smooth” like an eye test.

  • Panel by panel

    Panels and dialogue are detected automatically and shown one by one in reading order.

  • Translation

    Dialogue is translated on device; hold to see the original.

  • Paper & ink

    A calm paper tone, a shelf of real spines, and night reading that follows Dark Mode.

  • Reading diary

    What you read each day and for how long, with your best panel brought to life.

  • Privacy

    No data collected. Your library and reading history stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

The iPhone Duo layouts — outer screen, open spread and half-open tent — are tuned in the simulator today; on-device tuning starts in late November 2026.
