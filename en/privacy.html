Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-28

In short

Alicia Comic collects no data: no account, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs.

Your library and reading history

Stored only on your device and, if you turn on iCloud, in your own iCloud private database and iCloud Drive. The developer cannot see them.

Servers

Komga / OPDS addresses and accounts you add are stored on your device, passwords in the Keychain; the app talks only to the server you entered.

Weather

The reading diary’s weather comes from Apple’s WeatherKit, fetched only when you open the diary; your location is not stored.

Translation

Done on device by the system Translation framework; dialogue is never sent to the developer.

Diagnostics

Crash and performance reports stay on your device unless you export them yourself from Settings › About and send them to me. Sharing analytics with developers is your choice in system Settings.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new date.
