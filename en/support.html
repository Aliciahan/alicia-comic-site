Which formats can it read?

CBZ / ZIP, EPUB, PDF and image folders. It can also stream from your own Komga or OPDS server.

How do I add books?

Tap “+” at the top right of the shelf to import from Files; turn on Wi‑Fi transfer in Settings and drag books in from a computer’s browser; or add your server.

Does my library sync between devices?

Yes. With iCloud on, your library and reading history sync together through your own iCloud — no third-party server involved.

Why doesn’t HD start sometimes?

HD uses a lot of power, so it pauses when the phone is hot or in Low Power Mode; you can also limit it to charging in Settings › Quality. While charging overnight, the app prepares the pages you’ll read next.

What about iPhone Duo?

The outer screen, the open spread and the half-open tent each have their own layout. They are tuned in the simulator today; on-device tuning starts in late November 2026.

Does the app provide comics?

No. Import books you own or connect your own server.
